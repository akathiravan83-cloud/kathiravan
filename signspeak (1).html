SignSpeak AI

A limited gesture-recognition prototype for American Sign Language (ASL) static handshapes. It tracks your hand with MediaPipe, then classifies the handshape with a documented rule-based algorithm. It is not a full sign language translator: real signing also uses movement, facial expression, grammar and context.

How it works

  1. MediaPipe finds 21 hand landmarks in each webcam frame.
  2. The classifier checks which fingers are extended (distance from wrist, so it works at any hand rotation or size).
  3. The handshape is matched to a supported sign. No match shows “Unknown gesture”.
  4. A vote over the last 10 frames removes flicker.
  5. You confirm with Add to Sentence (or Space key).

Privacy: video stays in your browser. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. History is saved only in this browser’s localStorage.
